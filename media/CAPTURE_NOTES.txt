SUIKODEN II: REBALANCED v1.1 - FINAL GALLERY

16 standalone photos plus one 9:16 portrait collage containing eight selected
photos. All images were freshly captured from the final build in native Linux
PCSX-Redux. The photos-only collage is 1080 x 1920, with no added text,
logos, borders or padding. Eight genuine photos fill its entire canvas.
Nearest-neighbor resizing preserves source pixel colors. The large Greenhill
panel has a narrow side crop; the other seven panels retain their full frames.
Exact panel rectangles, resize dimensions and crop are in PROVENANCE.json.
The standalone images retain the original 320x240 game pixels, without any
painting, AI generation, retouching, sharpening, cropping or replacement.

Normal cold boots and controller input used private copies of downloaded
campaign cards. No game/party/inventory/story/RAM edits or state loads were
used to make these photos. Cards had the documented growth/slot migration;
their history before download is unknown. Some supplied saves have high stats
or maximum money. Those are checkpoint contents, not evidence of a continuous
campaign or new-game progression. KoRnholio/Suikosource campaign card sources
are identified by source URL and hash in the per-photo provenance.

The photos intentionally mix exploration, dialogue, equipment, currency,
personal-rune menus, battle continuation and victory. Later locations may be
spoilers. Image12 shows the maximum-money field menu after the Potch spacing
repair. Image13 shows complete blacksmith currency and price labels after the
constructor and later resize repairs. Image14 shows corrected Spider Slay help;
the recorded native route executes it successfully twice in the same battle.
Image15 shows Tuta's HP after automatic Medicine; its numeric trace establishes
95 -> 301 HP and one Mega Medicine charge consumed, 7 -> 6. It is not a capture
of the entire heal animation. Image16 shows ordinary native battle victory.
The four rune/Medicine capture routes complete victory and return to the field.

Snapshots and selected native routes do not certify every balance combination,
every dialogue rendering, a full uninterrupted campaign, or real hardware.
No ROM, BIOS, emulator, memory card, RAM dump or save state is included.
