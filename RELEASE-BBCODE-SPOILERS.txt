[CENTER][SIZE=5][COLOR=#368E7B][B]Install & Play[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE][/CENTER]

[CENTER][B]Requires your own untouched USA SLUS-00958 BIN/CUE[/B][/CENTER]

[LIST]
[*]Extract the download ZIP
[*]Check the original BIN size and SHA-256 against README.txt
[*][URL=https://github.com/jmacd/xdelta/releases]Apply the xdelta3 patch[/URL] to a clean original, using a new output BIN
[*]Copy the original CUE and update its FILE entry to the patched BIN
[*]Verify the patched hash, restart the emulator, and open the copied CUE
[/LIST]

[B]A new game is recommended[/B]
Vanilla saves keep their own growth/rune-slot table; disc patching does not migrate it or recalculate earned stats. Correctly migrated v0.99/v1.0 cards need no further table migration. Use normal saves instead of old emulator states.

Apply v1.1 to a clean original even when upgrading. The ZIP contains only the patch and two readmes. No ROM, BIOS, emulator, or save is supplied.
